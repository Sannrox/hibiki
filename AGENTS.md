# Repository guidance

- Read `VISION.md` and `docs/decisions/` before proposing implementation.
- Treat the accepted ADRs as the current MVP boundary; amend them explicitly
  before changing product ownership, safety, or learning semantics.
- Keep factual claims traceable to source artifacts.
- Never publish to X or mutate an external account without explicit approval.
- Keep secrets, tokens, analytics exports, and imported private material out of
  version control.
- Prefer narrow vertical slices and deterministic tests.
