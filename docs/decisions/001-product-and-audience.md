# ADR 001: Product and audience contract

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Hibiki helps one operator turn real Tenkai work into qualified reach that
attracts early users, testers, and collaborators. Its initial audience is the
first small group of developers and open-source maintainers already interested
in local-first agents, self-hosted AI tooling, or autonomous software
operations.

V0 covers only Tenkai. A source is eligible only when it:

- is public on Tenkai's GitHub default branch;
- represents a meaningful behavior or design change;
- has supporting tests, documentation, checks, or other concrete evidence;
- can be explained without private context;
- provides a useful takeaway beyond announcing a feature; and
- does not repeat a recent subject.

Chisei ranks eligible commits by usefulness, novelty, evidence strength, and
audience relevance. No candidate is a valid outcome when none passes.

Each proposal contains one standalone post in a direct first-person builder
voice: concrete, slightly opinionated, free of marketing filler, and bounded by
claims supported by the source evidence. V0 does not generate threads,
long-form posts, hashtags for reach, engagement bait, or multiple variants.

## Outcome

Relevant technical replies are the primary success signal. A reply counts when
it indicates a potential user, tester or contributor, or contains substantive
technical discussion. Impressions, likes, reposts, quotes, and follows are
diagnostic or secondary signals.
