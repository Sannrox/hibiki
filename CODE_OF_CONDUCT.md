# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Hibiki contributors should make participation respectful, safe, and useful.
Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable, and proportionate feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes; and
- protecting private or security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
managed by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose conduct violates
this standard.

For an ordinary disagreement, address the behavior in the relevant thread or
ask a maintainer to moderate. For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the
maintainer privately through the contact methods on the
[@Sannrox GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sannrox). Do not use the security
reporting channel unless the report is also a software security issue.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. The project
currently makes no guaranteed response-time commitment. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this code.
